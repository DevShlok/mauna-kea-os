### 4. Invoice Module Feedback Enhancements
- **Candidate-First Selection Flow**: Step 1 starts by selecting placed candidate(s), auto-resolving linked Mandate, Client, and signed Contract. Prevents orphan invoices.
- **Proposal Deck Fee Slab Auto-Matching**: Auto-calculates success fee % based on candidate CTC (`<50L`=18%, `50L-1Cr`=20%, `>1Cr`=25%).
- **Multi-Placement Line Items (`+ Add Line Item`)**: Supports billing multiple candidate placements on a single Tax Invoice.
- **100% Editable Particulars Description**: Line descriptions default to *"Executive Search Professional Fee — Success fee (20%) for Placement against Annual CTC of ₹50 Lakhs"* and are fully editable per line.
- **Precise Tax Regime Splits**: Support for `INTRA_STATE` (CGST 9% + SGST 9%), `UNION_TERRITORY` (CGST 9% + UTGST 9%), and `INTER_STATE` (IGST 18%).
- **Printable Layout Updates**: Displays multi-line item particulars, tax breakdown rows, and total amount in words.
